In a world cluttered with noise, finding a quiet corner on the internet feels like a luxury. We are constantly bombarded with notifications, pop-ups, and sticky headers clamoring for our attention.

## Digital Gardens

The concept of a digital garden—a personal space that is cultivated over time—appeals to me. It's not about performance or engagement metrics. It's about expression.

> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." - Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

I built this site to reflect that philosophy. Just text, typography, and thoughts.
