---
title: "Fruit of a mystery tree"
description: "for fun"
pubDate: 2025-06-16
tags: ["poetry"]
---

Fruit of a dark spring tree
I find along my path, resting by my feet.

Curious, I reach down
to see what sweetness nature holds.

I press my thumb in, gently, to peer into its tender heart.

But the flesh gives too quickly—
the skin breaks in violence,
my thumb left still
in a wash of blood red.
