I picked up this small paperback book while waiting at a bookstore off 24th and Mission. It was at an unlisted bookstore, "unlisted" because this address classifies itself as a small art gallery, though the storefront has plenty a variety of used books. I think this was the best curated "bookstore" that I have yet to see, inside the city and out, and I was able to find a number of books (on my want-to-read) that I had never seen elsewhere.

I wish I had purchased this book while I was there (waiting). I personally have never read a published book quite like it. It was almost like a zine, the way it felt so unpolished yet intentional. Nothing about the concept is ordinarily so interesting to me, yet it was extremely well done and I could not put it down for the eventual 30 minutes I spent waiting for my friend to arrive.

It felt like a fever dream, soap opera, and an intimate diary. If anyone wants to buy me this $10 book so I can read the last thirty of fifty pages I'd simply thank you.
