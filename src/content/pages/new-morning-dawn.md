---
title: "In the morning dawn"
description: "morning commute"
pubDate: 2025-02-18
tags: ["poetry"]
---

In the new morning's dawn, 
street torches alight, 
saluting with the trees–
the moon watches still, 
her sleepy eyed guard 
over man's steady breath:
a Child's yawn, a civilization emerging.

Ecology peers out 
through touched earth, 
humming with a curious purple glow—
colors which only exist for a moment, 
colors our eyes cannot carry.
