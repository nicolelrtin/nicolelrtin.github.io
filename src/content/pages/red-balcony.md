title: "The Red Balcony by Jonathan Wilson"
description: "Book Review. A political thriller, mystery, and philosophy. 4/5."
pubDate: 2025-07-07
tags: ["review", "books"]
---

I came to The Red Balcony with little prior knowledge of the time period, and instead learned more about the specific political context in discussions outside of the book. The Red Balcony well reflected the questions raised; it was politically rich and thought-provoking. It explored the relevant controversies in Zionist divisions, British imperialism, and the silence around Arab resistance. 

The novel offered a compelling fictional narrative storyline through the Arlosoroff controversy, thereby exploring tensions between pragmatism, jewish identity, and colonial rise in the face of fascism. I appreciated the historical accuracy and how well it explored the questions I was still starting to ask; I often couldn’t understand the book’s nuances fully as I was still understanding the conflict itself. 

It had political thriller, mystery, and philosophy. Probably best read some familiarity with the subject beforehand, as it isn’t slow to explain. The narrator, Ivor Castle, is a bit of a tool, but the depth and process of reading this book was interesting enough for me to ignore narrative criticisms.
